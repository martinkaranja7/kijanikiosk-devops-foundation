#why i choose the PAAS Cloud Service Model 

I selected  Platform as a Service (PaaS) while utilizing Containers

When doing the initial launch of the KijaniKiosk platform, i think Platform as a Service model e.g AWS Elastic Beanstalk will be ideal, for the following reasons

1. Focus on the core of the  Business 
during the early stages much effort should be directed toward developing customer facing features rather than managing the  infrastructure e.g servers and virtual machines.

 2. Simplicity of the setup
IaaS will require  manual setup of computer, load balancer,and OS maintenance while with  PaaS ability to scale increase load balancers and health checks is automated.
